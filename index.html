How I work

I own LLM-powered agentic workflows end-to-end, from an ambiguous business problem to production agents that reason, plan, and act across tools, APIs, and sensitive enterprise data. At the AI Accelerator Hub I ship multi-agent strategy pipelines, RAG-grounded platforms, and real-time systems in clean, testable, observable Python and TypeScript, with evaluation frameworks that measure accuracy, safety, and latency instead of trial-and-error.

The second half of my practice is security: SOC monitoring and incident response with SIEM tooling, vulnerability management, and controls mapped to NIST CSF, ISO 27001, and ITSG-33, plus continuous offensive research on HackerOne, Bugcrowd, Intigriti, and Immunefi (IDOR/BOLA, SSRF, auth bypass, cloud misconfiguration) and LLM red-teaming against prompt injection, data exfiltration, and agent misuse. I build systems the way an attacker reads them.
